About CivitasX

CivitasX is an independent student-led initiative focused on quantitative finance, software engineering, mathematical modeling, and data-driven research.

Our objective is to investigate complex financial systems through computational methods, mathematical reasoning, and independent research.

Research projects should document their methodology, assumptions, data sources, limitations, and individual contributions.
